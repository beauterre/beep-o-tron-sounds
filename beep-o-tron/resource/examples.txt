{"o":[
{"t":3,"f":"440.000","v":"1","c":-1
,"ve":[{"t":0,"v":0},
{"t":1,"v":1.0}]

Example of a volume envelope on a modifier (you need to give it a bit of amplitude, so we should multiply with the v given!)
{"o":[
{"t":3,"f":"440.000","v":"1","c":-1},
{"t":2,"f":"11","v":"1","c":0
,"ve":[{"t":0,"v":1},
{"t":1,"v":800.0}]
}],
"l":1.5,
"e":[{"t":0,"v":1},{"t":1,"v":1}]}
